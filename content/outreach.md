# MaTh.en.JEANS

[MaTh.en.JEANS](https://www.mathenjeans.fr/) is a french non-profit organization proposing to middle- and highschool students to do mathematical research during their school year. 
Each groups of 4 or 5 students, works on a subject that is designed by a mathematician that works in academia. Towards the end of the school year, the students who followed the program have the opportunity to present their findings at a regional MaTh.en.JEANS congress in front of other students, teachers and mathematicians.
