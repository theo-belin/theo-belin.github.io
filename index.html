Théo Belin

Since May 2026, I am a postdoctoral researcher at the LTH faculty of the Center for Mathematical Sciences at Lund University, under the supervision of Gabriele Brüll. Before that, from Februray 2025 until March 2026, I held a postdoctoral position at the IADM at the University of Stuttgart with Guido Schneider. I completed a PhD between 2021 and 2024 at the Fédération de Mathématiques de CentraleSupélec under the supervision of Pauline Lafitte.

Research

I am interested in the analysis of partial differential equations (PDEs), in particular the dynamics of thin film equations, modulation equations for diffusive and dispersive PDEs, degenerate parabolic equations, geometric evolution equations and numerical analysis with finite volumes.
